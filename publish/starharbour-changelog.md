## First release — the station wakes

Star Harbour has been dark a long time. Its cats are still aboard, and they
know the corridors better than the last harbourmaster ever did.

**A glass dome** looking out on deep space, with the beds the cats sleep in —
each one carrying a name — and the harbourmaster's log waiting in the chest.

**A sealed corridor** east to the hangar, lit by ribbons in the ceiling.

**The hangar.** A shuttle that never flew again, with something still strapped
in its hold, and two spear-fighters beside it that are older still. Nobody
remembers who flew them.

**The observation deck** at the top of the station. Take the service ladder up
through the hatch; the stars are closest there.

**Four energy blades**, one of each colour, locked away in different bays
around the station. They cut, and they light your way.

**Purrfect Companions is built in** — every cat, outfit and piece of furniture
from the add-on works here, with nothing to enable by hand.

Four tasks, in order, in the log. And a fifth cat that the log will only hint
at.
