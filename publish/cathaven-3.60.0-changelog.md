## Cat Haven 3.60.0 — More to do in the north village

- Cat Cafe: cook your catch in a smoker, craft cat treats and picnic at the tables. A supply chest includes cod, wheat, sugar, coal and treats.
- Cart Depot: equip a tamed adult cat with one of three cart colors and ride the wide gravel loop. Carts now attach to a visible harness. This is a practice route, without a race timer.
- Fishing Grove: a small pond with a pier and shallow banks, plus fishing rods, saplings, bone meal and picnic supplies.
- New signs and three handbook activity pages connect the new areas to the existing garden and trading post.
- Includes the recent spa, TV and scratching-board fixes. Grass and ordinary flowers no longer prevent scratching-board visits.

The existing quests, cats and courtyard remain available. Packaged-world checks passed all 397 landmarks and path checks, and the existing simulated-player playthrough passed. Manual client playtesting of the new activities remains pending.

This download contains the public English world with Purrfect Companions 3.60.0 embedded. Import creates a new world copy; it does not update an existing save in place.
