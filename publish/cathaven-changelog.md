## The world grew — a lot

Everything below is new since the first Cat Haven release.

**The Cat Parkour.** A lantern-lit wooden course east of the crystal cave.
Ride a cat up the ramp and jump platform to platform — the last stretch is
wide gaps and single-block landings. A chest of golden wings waits at the end.

**The deep lake.** Far east, past the meadow and the mountain. Dive to the
bottom, swim the tunnel, hold your breath — a trident is waiting in the dark.

**The attic.** The old caretaker's best gear never left the house. Getting up
there takes some looking; the diary in the cellar knows the way.

**The trading post.** A backpack cat digs up string, feathers and the odd
diamond. Now there is a barrel that takes them — and the first trade brings
out the caretaker's old cape.

**Superpowers.** Every one of the eighteen outfits now does something. The
cape makes a ridden cat faster and higher-jumping, leaving a trail of stars in
the air. Wings mean no fall damage. The crown glows through walls. The doctor
coat heals. Fifteen more besides.

**The world opens as you play.** Paths start closed, with a sign saying which
quest opens them. Finish the lighthouse and the meadow road opens; find the
three keys and the parkour path opens; beat the parkour and the lake grate
lifts. The story now has an order.

**A forest all the way around.** Oak, dark oak, spruce and birch frame the
whole map, with rabbits, foxes and sheep out among the trees.

**Kittens come in litters** of two or three, each named after its parent, and
sometimes born wearing a collar.

**The cats got cuter** — bigger ears, a glint in the eye, pink paw pads.

**Also:** a garden and tool shed behind spawn, six rainbow ribbons hidden in
places you already visit, a mountain to climb, lantern-lit paths, and rewards
(XP and items) on nearly every quest.

**Included as a world template too**, so you can start a fresh Cat Haven
whenever you like without re-importing anything.
