Purrfect Companions

Six hand-made cats for Minecraft Bedrock

They are not reskins. Each cat has its own colouring, size, temperament and voice — modelled after real breeds: two Siberians, a Sacred Birman, a Ragdoll, a Norwegian Forest Cat and a tuxedo shorthair.

What they do

  • Moods — a happy cat carries its tail high; feed a Cat Treat to cheer them up
  • Naps — they curl up and doze off purring; sitting cats purr with hearts
  • Tame them with cod, salmon or a crafted Cat Treat
  • Ride them once saddled — or hitch the cart and sit IN it, with cargo space and a seat for a friend
  • Cat fishing — a saddled cat in water catches cod for you
  • She carries — a backpack cat has fifteen slots, picks up what you drop or mine, and hands it over when you sneak up beside her
  • Treasure — backpack cats dig up string, feathers and the odd diamond
  • She warns you — a cat at your side bristles when something hostile closes in
  • They live together — cats groom each other, kittens play, and at night the flock curls up in a sleeping pile on the beds
  • Breed for kittens — sometimes born wearing a little bow
  • Achievements — sixteen to earn, from First Friend to Lighthouse Keeper… and a few we will not name
  • They seek out the bed, yarn ball, food bowl, litter box, cat tower, cardboard box, fish pond and cat door

And a cat suit for you

Four pieces — a hood with ears, a vest, trousers and paws — in four tiers: leather, iron, diamond and netherite. You do not re-craft it. You upgrade the piece you already wear by combining it with the metal, so the hood you made on your first day is the hood you still have in netherite.

  • Night vision in the hood, because cats see in the dark — netherite adds fire resistance
  • Resistance in the vest and speed in the trousers, both stepping up at diamond
  • A soft landing in the paws: no fall damage, and jump strength from iron upwards
  • The whole suit — speed II, a higher jump, and every cat around you purrs and heals

A suit is only as strong as its weakest piece: wear leather with netherite and you get the leather bonus. Protection runs from iron-grade to a little beyond diamond.

How to install

  • Open the .mcaddon file — Minecraft imports it automatically
  • Enable both packs (behaviour + resources) in your world
  • Find a cat, or use a spawn egg in Creative
  • Works on Minecraft Bedrock 1.20+ · no experimental toggles required
