2.3.2 — Readable button prompts

Standing next to a cat, or riding one, showed a raw translation key instead of a word. Minecraft builds the dismount prompt from the entity id, and there is no built-in text behind it for custom creatures. Every prompt the add-on can produce is now spelled out: *Put on*, *Ride*, *Mount*, *Dismount*.

2.3.1 — Snow's ears

Snow is a white cat, so her ears disappeared into the rest of her coat. They are now grey with a pink inner, clearly separated from the fur.
